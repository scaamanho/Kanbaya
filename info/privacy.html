Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the Kanbaya website and application maintained through the Kanbaya project. Kanbaya is a local-first Kanban application: no Kanbaya account or application backend is required to manage your projects.

1. Data stored on your device

Your boards, cards, descriptions, checklists, comments, labels, dates, templates and attachments are stored in your browser's IndexedDB database. Browser storage also holds application preferences and installation state, and the service worker caches application files for offline use.

Kanbaya does not automatically upload your projects to the maintainer or synchronize them between devices. Storage is specific to the browser profile and website origin. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles or browser storage eviction can remove your local projects.

2. Optional Google Drive access

Google Drive is optional. Opening a Drive backup dialog loads Google Identity Services from Google. Connecting then opens Google's authorization flow. Kanbaya does not request or receive your Google password.

Kanbaya requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, a permission limited to files created by the app or explicitly made available to it. This integration lists only backups it creates; it does not browse your whole Drive or request your email, contacts or Google profile.

  • Save: when you choose to save, Kanbaya sends a backup of all boards, templates and attachments directly from your browser to your Google Drive, in a folder named Kanbaya Backups.
  • List: Kanbaya reads file identifiers, names and modification times for its backup files so you can select a backup.
  • Import: when you select a backup and import it, Kanbaya downloads its contents directly to your browser and adds local copies. Existing boards are not replaced.

Google access tokens are held only in the page's memory, not in IndexedDB, localStorage, sessionStorage or backup files. Reloading the application clears that in-memory session. Closing the backup dialog does not sign you out of Google or revoke your authorization.

Google user data is used only to provide these backup and restore functions. Kanbaya does not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. The integration does not send tokens or backup contents to a maintainer-operated server.

Kanbaya's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's processing of data is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Hosting and other external requests

Kanbaya's code does not include analytics or advertising trackers. This does not prevent hosting providers or external services from processing connection information when your browser contacts them.

  • When the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process IP addresses and request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The application's home screen loads the support-button image from Buy Me a Coffee's CDN. That request exposes ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to that provider. Following the support link opens Buy Me a Coffee, which applies its own privacy and payment terms. Kanbaya does not process payment details.
  • External images in Markdown descriptions and attachments added from a URL contact the servers hosting those resources. Those servers may receive your IP address and request metadata. Downloaded attachments are then stored locally.
  • Google sign-in and Drive operations contact Google and may use Google's cookies or account session according to Google's policies. Drive requires an Internet connection; local editing and file backups do not require Google access.

4. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Local projects: remove individual items or use Delete all data to remove boards and their attachments. That command keeps templates and the theme. Clear this website's browser storage to remove all local application data and caches.
  • Downloaded backups: these remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Deleting a board does not delete exported files.
  • Drive backups: each save creates a separate file. You control retention through Google Drive, including its trash. Deleting local data or revoking permission does not automatically delete Drive backups; deleting a Drive backup does not remove boards already imported.
  • Google authorization: remove Kanbaya's access from your Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access; local features remain available.

You can export your local projects and attachments through the data menu. Because the maintainer has no backend copy of those projects, the maintainer cannot retrieve, edit or delete your local database or your private Drive backups for you.

5. Protecting your data

Google API transfers use HTTPS. Kanbaya does not add password protection or application-level encryption to its local database or backup files; device, browser and storage-provider protections may apply. Anyone who obtains a backup may be able to read its contents. Keep your device, Google account and backups secure, and avoid sharing files containing private information.

No browser storage, cloud service or software can guarantee against data loss or unauthorized access. Keep independent backups of important work.

6. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or requests concerning the maintainer's handling of information, contact the project maintainer through the Kanbaya issue tracker. Issues are public: do not attach private boards, backups, access tokens or account details.

If this policy changes, the updated text and date will be published on this page. Review it when enabling new optional integrations.
